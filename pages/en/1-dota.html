Game description

Dota 2 is a team-based multiplayer online MOBA where two teams of five players battle on a symmetrical map. Each side aims to destroy the opponent's main building, known as the Ancient. Players control unique heroes with different abilities, gain experience and gold, buy items, and work together tactically. Dota 2 is known for its high difficulty, huge hero roster, strategic depth, and no gameplay-affecting purchases.
