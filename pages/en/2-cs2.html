Game description

CS2 is a team-based first-person tactical shooter where counter-terrorists and terrorists fight rounds on specialized maps. Teams complete objectives such as planting a bomb, holding hostages, preventing an explosion, or rescuing hostages. Players earn money, buy weapons and armor, and use grenades for tactical control. CS2 is known for its competitive system, redesigned smoke physics, and no gameplay-affecting donations.
