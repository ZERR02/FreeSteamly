Game description

TF2 is a team-based class shooter where players choose one of nine unique classes and fight across varied maps. Depending on the mode, teams capture flags, hold control points, deliver a bomb cart, or defend their base. Players use unique weapons and abilities, coordinate with medics, and strengthen their team through tactical play. TF2 is known for its cartoon style, huge loot arsenal, humor, and no gameplay-affecting donations.
